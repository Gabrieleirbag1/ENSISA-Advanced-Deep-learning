What do the lines mean?

Lines on the PCA plot show which chunk is being compared with the query. They do not represent the distance used for retrieval. Similarities and top-k ranks are calculated from the original embedding vectors, not from 2D PCA.

What can we conclude?

Why can the rankings differ?

Dimensionality reduction: PCA retains only two principal components, discarding information from the remaining dimensions.

Similarity metric: Euclidean distance measures geometric proximity, whereas cosine similarity measures the angle between vectors.

Important: PCA centers embeddings before projection. Consequently, comparing cosine similarities before and after PCA also involves a change of origin.

The actual RAG retrieval always uses the original 384-dimensional embeddings.

Each point represents a document chunk. The projection is illustrative and does not replace full-dimensional similarity.
